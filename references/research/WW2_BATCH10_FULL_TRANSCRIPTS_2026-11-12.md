## 1. P-47N and M performance summaries

Sources: Republic Aviation Report ES-300 (P-47M) and ES-302-a (P-47N); public comparison tables.

**Comparison table (approximate public values):**

**P-47M notes (ES-300):** Guaranteed high speed 462 mph at 38 750 ft with 2100 BHP; service figures commonly cited ~470–480 mph at altitude.  
**P-47N notes:** Heavier due to wing tanks; high speed ~423 mph at military power (38 000 ft); combat radius significantly larger.

## 2. AFDU tactical trials on P-47

Source: AFDU / AFDS comparative assessments (early 1945 and earlier P-47C trials).

Key quantitative notes:  
- Tempest II vs Thunderbolt: Tempest II ~80 mph faster at low levels (to 2000 ft), advantage reducing to 40 mph by 20 000 ft; parity ~28 000 ft; Thunderbolt ~20 mph faster at 31 000 ft.  
- Tempest II out-turned the Thunderbolt at any height (wing loading 38 vs 49 lb/sq.ft).  
- Earlier P-47C trials at Duxford (winter 1943) used for comparative work against contemporary types.

## 4. Configuration-matched cooling and power notes

- Water injection (ADI) and higher manifold pressures (up to 70") cleared with temperature limits on extended climbs.  
- P-47N wing tanks and increased mass reduce performance relative to lighter M at equal power.  
- Cooling state (cowl flaps, intercoolers) must be matched when comparing climb points across D / M / N.
