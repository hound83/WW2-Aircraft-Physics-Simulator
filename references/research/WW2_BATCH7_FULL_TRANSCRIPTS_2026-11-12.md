# Batch 7 full transcripts (public sources)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Available public transcriptions of Mustang B/C high-blower and drag items (Wright Field 43-24777, 43-6883, 43-12093; NACA 4K02 / L-566).  
**Note:** Full original Wright Field tables are on wwiiaircraftperformance.org; quantitative content is captured below. NACA reports are US Government works.

## 1. Wright Field 43-24777 high-blower level and climb (with/without racks)

Source: https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/p51b-24777.html (P-51B-15-NA, V-1650-7, 44-1 fuel)

**Level speed (3000 RPM):**  
- High blower critical altitude for 75 in Hg: 20 800 ft → 444.0 mph (without wing racks)  
- With wing racks (67" Hg): ~364 mph SL; high blower ~426 mph at 23 900 ft  
- With wing racks (75" Hg): ~380 mph SL; high blower ~431 mph at 20 600 ft  
- Without wing racks (75" Hg): ~388 mph SL; high blower 444 mph at 20 600 ft  

**Climb (approximate from related summaries):**  
- Low blower max: ~3920 ft/min at 5600 ft (67") / ~4380 ft/min at 2300 ft (75")  
- High blower max: ~3170 ft/min at 19 200 ft (67") / ~3700 ft/min at 15 600 ft (75")  

**Notes:** Gross weight ~9680 lb. Oil and coolant shutters in automatic. High speeds from faired curve average of level flight tests.

## 2. Wright Field 43-6883 and 43-12093 full altitude tables

Source: https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/p-51b-6883.html (and related)

**43-6883 (67" Hg, 3000 RPM):**  
- Low blower critical: 430.0 mph at 16 600 ft  
- High blower critical: 442.0 mph at 29 400 ft  
- Sea level: 371.0 mph  
- Climb: 3450 ft/min at 13 800 ft (low); 2660 ft/min at 25 500 ft (high)  
- Time to high blower critical altitude (25 500 ft): ~8.28 min  

**43-12093:** Referenced in NAA performance calculations (NA-5534); full public table transcription thinner in this pass. Used as a B/C comparison specimen.

## 3. NACA ACR 4K02 prop-off drag polar points

Source: Related NACA profile-drag investigations on XP-51 (ACR 245 / later reports).  

Key notes:  
- Prop-off drag measurements on the XP-51 laminar-flow wing.  
- Low-drag airfoil section; transition and profile drag data available in NACA reports.  
- Full polar points are in the original NACA documents (US Government works); public HTML transcriptions are limited.

## 4. NACA WR L-566 XP-51 stall and mass notes

Source: NACA WR L-566 (April 1943), "Flying Qualities and Stalling Characteristics of North American XP-51 Airplane" (White, Hoover, Garris).  

Key notes:  
- First airplane tested at Langley with low-drag airfoil section.  
- Stalling characteristics: requirements met except for stall warning.  
- Short-period longitudinal oscillation disappeared within one cycle.  
- Mass and CG data included in the original report; specific numerical stall CAS/EAS values are in the full document.

## 5. Cooling-flap and high-blower condition notes

From Wright Field tests:  
- Oil and coolant shutters in automatic or specified positions (closed for high speed).  
- High-blower critical altitudes explicitly recorded (e.g., 20 800 ft at 75" on 43-24777).  
- Wing racks add measurable drag (~10–20 mph depending on altitude and power).
