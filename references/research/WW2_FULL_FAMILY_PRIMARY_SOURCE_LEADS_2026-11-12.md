# Full-family primary source leads (consolidated)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Purpose:** Single-view index of the highest-value primary source archives and report series for the major WWII fighter families. Supports expansion beyond the current 13 variants.  
**Status:** Research leads only. Every number still requires specimen, boost, cooling, mass, and intake matching before use.

## Spitfire family (all marks)
- **A&AEE Boscombe Down** performance trials (primary):
  - K5054 (prototype handling/performance)
  - BS.543 / BS.551 (F/LF/HF IX, Tables II/V — Merlin 61/66/70)
  - MA.648 (LF IX, Table I — different intake/prop)
  - JL.165 (+25 lb / 150-grade)
  - EN409 (RAE high-Mach with Mustang I)
  - Seafire IIC / L IIC / III / XV / 47 series
- **RAE Farnborough:** ARC R&M 2535 (high-speed tunnel models of Spitfire, Spiteful, Attacker, Mustang); aileron and compressibility work.
- **AFDU:** Tactical trials vs contemporary opponents.
- **Key public anchors:** BS.543 climbs (~4620 ft/min SL Merlin 66), MA.648 379 mph at 9400 ft MS. Keep boost and specimen separate.
- **Priority gap:** Near-stall CAS/EAS with instrument correction and run mass for comparison marks.

## Bf 109 family (all variants)
- **Erprobungsstelle Rechlin:**
  - Kennblatt Bf 109 G-1 with DB 605A (Erprobungsnummer 1586, early 1943)
  - Vergleichsfliegen Bf 109 F-4 vs Fw 190 A-2 (10 December 1941 / 15023/41) — climb inferiority of A-2 by ~6 min to 10 000 m; speed near parity or slight deficit at altitude
  - Vergleichsfliegen Bf 109 E vs Spitfire / Hurricane / Curtiss (August 1940)
  - GM-1 trials (E-series, +60–105 km/h at 8–11 km)
  - Kennblätter for F-1/F-2 (DB 601 N)
- **Finnish trials:** MT-215 / WNr 14783 (5 April 1943) — actual 2540 rpm vs 2580 indicated, 1.30–1.32 ata below critical height; climb from 5–10 km.
- **Soviet NII VVS:** WNr 14513 gun-pod; 1944 compilation (clean vs gunpod masses).
- **Kurfürst archive:** Consolidated transcriptions of the above.
- **Priority gap:** Installed DB 601 E / DB 605 A power-by-altitude at documented restricted combat settings; matched run mass.

## Fw 190 family (all variants)
- **Focke-Wulf Flugberichte:** WNr 783 series (Nr. 1 long external intake, combat 2400 rpm / 1.32 ata; open/closed cooling climb rows already extracted). Additional A-5/A-6/A-8/A-9 and D-9/D-11 reports.
- **Rechlin Vergleichsfliegen:** A-2 vs Bf 109 F-4.
- **Manufacturer / E-Stelle:** BMW 801 D power anchors, internal vs external intake curves, MW 50 campaigns.
- **Priority gap:** Direct propeller efficiency or useful-power measurements; graded open-cooling area; D-9 dry / MW 50 kept separate.

## Mustang family (all variants)
- **Wright Field:** 43-24777 (B-15) level and climb (already used). Additional P-51B/C/D/H and Allison-powered series on wwiiaircraftperformance.org.
- **NACA:** ACR 4K02 (prop-off drag correlation), WR L-566 (XP-51 stall/handling).
- **A&AEE / RAE:** Mustang I AG393 high-Mach trials with Spitfire XI; Mustang III (Merlin 100) reports.
- **Priority gap:** High-blower matched power and near-stall corrections for the B/C specimens used in comparisons.

## P-47 family (all variants)
- **Wright Field / NACA:** L4L07 / L5G09a (B/C propeller efficiency — already core). ACR 5D20 (D-model compressibility), WR L-86 (profile drag), P-47D-30 stall RM.
- **AFDU / Eglin:** Tactical trials (P-47C).
- **Additional:** P-47M / P-47N performance comparisons, water-injection reports.
- **Priority gap:** D-30 and later military-power climb and high-speed with matched cooling and tip-Mach.

## Related families (for expansion beyond 13)
- **P-38:** Wright Field FS-M-19-1538-A (42-12687) already used; additional G/J/L power and propeller comparison memoranda.
- **F4U / F6F:** BuAer production inspection trials, NACA stall/handling, A&AEE Hellcat and Corsair reports.
- **P-40:** Manufacturer and AAF trials; limited NACA performance.
- **Japanese (Ki-84 etc.):** TAIC Summary Reports and captured-aircraft tests.
