## 1. NACA WR L-86 full profile-drag and transition data

Source: NACA WR L-86 / ACR L5H11a (October 1945), Zalovcik — Flight Investigation of Boundary-Layer and Profile-Drag Characteristics of Smooth Wing Sections of a P-47D Airplane.

Key quantitative points:  
- Mach range 0.25–0.69.  
- Minimum profile-drag coefficient 0.0062 for smooth section at 63% semi-span.  
- Critical Mach number exceeded by 0.04 with no compressibility shocks observed.  
- Boundary-layer transition as far back as 20% chord on upper surface at low lift coefficients (in slipstream).  
- Measurements at 25% and 63% semi-span; Reynolds numbers 7.7×10⁶ to 19.7×10⁶.

## 3. P-47D-30 stall RM quantitative points

Source: NACA flight tests on P-47D-30 longitudinal stability and stalling characteristics.

Key notes:  
- Ample stall warning in all conditions tested.  
- Stalling characteristics very satisfactory except in approach and wave-off conditions.  
- Power-on clean: mild buffeting ~4 mph above stall; initial right roll then left, controllable.  
- Accelerated flight (power-on clean): mild lateral instability, easily controlled with ailerons.  
- Elevator-trim force changes due to power and flaps small and satisfactory.

## 4. Wright Field water-injection and military-power climb for D-series

Source: Wright Field tests on P-47D (e.g., 42-26167 and related), 44-1 fuel and water injection.

**With water injection:**  
- 70" Hg: 444 mph at 23 200 ft  
- 65" Hg: 439 mph at 25 200 ft; climb 3260 ft/min at 10 000 ft  

**Without water:**  
- 65" Hg: 430 mph at 25 400 ft; climb 2850 ft/min at 12 000 ft  
- 56" Hg: 418 mph at 29 600 ft; climb 2330 ft/min at 12 000 ft  

**Notes:** No detonation observed in level flight up to 65" without water / 70" with water. Climbs limited by cylinder head and carburetor air temperatures. Water tank capacity increased to 30 gallons on later D-series (~15 min WEP).

---

## 5. Tip-Mach and cooling-state notes for the above

- Water injection (ADI) allowed higher manifold pressures with acceptable temperatures in level flight.  
- Climb duration limited by cooling; short climbs possible at high power.  
- Tip-Mach effects not fully quantified in the public Wright Field summaries for these specific tests.
