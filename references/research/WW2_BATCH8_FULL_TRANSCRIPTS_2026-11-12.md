# Batch 8 full transcripts (public sources)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Available public transcriptions of Mustang additional items (P-51D 44-15342, Mustang III / Merlin 100, Allison series, A&AEE/RAE high-Mach notes).  
**Note:** Full original Wright Field and A&AEE tables are on wwiiaircraftperformance.org; quantitative content is captured below.

## 1. P-51D (44-15342) full Wright Field tables

Source: https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/p51d-15342.html (Memo TSCEP5E-1908, 15 June 1945)

**Clean configuration (with bomb racks):**  
- High blower War Emergency (3000 RPM / 67"): 442 mph at 26 000 ft  
- High blower Military (3000 RPM / 61"): 439 mph at 28 000 ft  
- Low blower War Emergency: 417 mph at 10 000 ft  
- Sea level War Emergency: 375 mph  
- Climb table points available (e.g., ~3600 ft/min at SL under WEP conditions)  

**Notes:** Additional configurations (two 110 gal tanks, bombs) flown at 5000 and 15 000 ft. Gross weight ~9700 lb class.

---

## 2. A&AEE / RAE Mustang I AG393 high-Mach trials

Source: Public summaries of RAE Farnborough compressibility work on Mustang I / P-51D.

Key quantitative notes:  
- Mustang I dives limited by altitude performance; max Mach ~0.80 from ~28 000 ft start.  
- Later P-51D trials: several dives to Mach 0.84 (one to 0.85); structural damage in heavy buffet (buckled flap skin, cracked radiator, broken hydraulic line).  
- Recommendation: restrict to Mach 0.80. No tuck-under tendency with power changes. Elevator trim not required for recovery.

## 3. Mustang III (Merlin 100) reports

Source: http://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/fx858.html (FX.858, take-off weight 9260 lb)

**At 3000 rpm / +25 lb/sq.in.:**  
- Max rate of climb MS gear: 4500 ft/min at 1600 ft  
- Max rate of climb FS gear: 4000 ft/min at 13 000 ft  
- Service ceiling (100 ft/min): 39 800 ft  
- Absolute ceiling: 40 700 ft  
- Max speed MS gear: 419 mph at 5200 ft  
- Max speed FS gear: 455 mph at 17 800 ft  

**Notes:** Use of +25 instead of +18 increases speed below full throttle heights by ~26 mph and climb by ~850–900 ft/min. Propeller tip Mach >0.9 over most of the height range.

## 4. Allison-powered P-51 series performance tables

Source: https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/mustang-I.html and related.

**Examples (XP-51 / Mustang I):**  
- High speed ~375–384 mph at 16 500–19 000 ft (present/future military rating)  
- Max rate of climb ~2720 ft/min  
- Time to 20 000 ft ~8.80 min  
- Cleaned-up conditions on early specimens reached ~388.5 mph
