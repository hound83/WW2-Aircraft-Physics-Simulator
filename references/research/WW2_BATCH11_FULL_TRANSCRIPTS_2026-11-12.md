# Batch 11 full transcripts (public sources)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Available public transcriptions of related families for expansion (Wright Field P-38, BuAer F4U/F6F, TAIC Japanese).  
**Note:** Full original BuAer and TAIC tables are on wwiiaircraftperformance.org and related archives; quantitative content available publicly is captured below. Configuration-matched conditions are flagged where incomplete.

---

## 1. Wright Field P-38 performance tables

Source: Engineering Division Memorandum Report Eng-47-1706-A (P-38J, February 1944) and related public summaries.

**P-38J (60" Hg / 3000 rpm, take-off weight ~16 597 lb):**  
- Max speed SL: 345.0 mph  
- Max speed at critical altitude (25 800 ft): 421.5 mph  
- Rate of climb SL: 4000 ft/min  
- Rate of climb at critical altitude (23 400 ft): 2900 ft/min  
- Time to critical altitude: 6.49 min  
- Service ceiling: 40 000 ft  

**Notes:** Comparative tests also run against P-47D-10, P-39Q-5, and P-51B. High-altitude performance strong; dive and acrobatic limits noted.

## 2. BuAer F4U / F6F performance

Source: BuAer data sheets and public performance trials (wwiiaircraftperformance.org).

**F6F examples:**  
- Max rate of climb (Main supercharger): ~2260 ft/min at 5400 ft  
- Max TAS (max permissible power): ~371 mph at 18 700 ft (Aux. Low)  
- With water injection: climb improvements noted (e.g., ~3160 ft/min in some ranges)  
- F6F-5 vs Zeke 52 (TAIC-linked trials): F6F-5 much faster at all altitudes (41 mph SL, up to ~75 mph at 25 000 ft); Zeke superior in slow-speed turns  

**F4U examples:**  
- Later marks (F4U-4) commonly cited ~446 mph; earlier marks lower.  
- Climb rates vary by mark and power setting; water injection used on some.  
- BuAer sheets provide configuration-specific speed and climb; full polar and stall points thinner in public HTML.

## 3. TAIC Japanese aircraft reports

Source: TAIC Manual No. 1 and combat evaluation reports (e.g., Zeke 52).

**Zeke 52 key points from Allied flight tests:**  
- Top speed typically ~335 mph at ~18 000 ft (test aircraft).  
- Climb: Zeke superior to F6F-5 below ~9000 ft, parity ~14 000 ft, F6F superior above.  
- Best climb speed: Zeke ~105 kt IAS vs F6F ~130 kt.  
- Rolls: equal below 200 kt, inferior above due to control forces.  
- Turns: Zeke greatly superior in slow-speed turns at low/medium altitude.  

**Other TAIC subjects** (Oscar, Tony, Frank, etc.): quantitative pages exist in the full Manual No. 1; public HTML extracts are limited.

## 4. Configuration-matched power and stall for expansion beyond the 13

- Power settings (manifold pressure, RPM, water injection) must be matched when comparing across families.  
- Stall CAS/EAS with instrument corrections remain sparse for most expansion types in public sources.  
- Mass and cooling state (radiators, cowl flaps) are explicit variables in the Wright Field and BuAer reports.
