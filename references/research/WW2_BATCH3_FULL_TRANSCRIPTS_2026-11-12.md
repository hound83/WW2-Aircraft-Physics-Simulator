## 1. A&AEE BS.543 (Spitfire F.Mk.IX, Merlin 66 / +18 lb)

Source: Public summaries on spitfireperformance.com and related A&AEE references (4 March 1943).

**Climb (radiators open where noted):**  
- Rate of climb at sea level: 4620 ft/min  
- Rate of climb at full throttle height in M.S. gear: 4700 ft/min at 7000 ft  
- Rate of climb at full throttle height in F.S. gear: 3860 ft/min at 18 000 ft  
- Rate of climb at 30 000 ft: 2125 ft/min  
- Time to 10 000 ft: 2.15 min  
- Time to 20 000 ft: 4.75 min  
- Time to 30 000 ft: 8.4 min  
- Height at which rate of climb is 1000 ft/min: 37 100 ft  

**Level speed (radiators shut, corrected to 95% take-off weight):**  
- M.S. gear: ~384 mph  
- F.S. gear: ~407 mph  

**Notes:**  
BS.543 was a converted Mk V with an experimental propeller that produced above-average full throttle heights. Results corrected to standard atmosphere by A&AEE/Res/170 methods. Climb based on all-up weights; level speed corrected to 95% take-off weight. Mean weight around 7450 lb.

## 2. A&AEE JL.165 (+25 lb / 150-grade)

Source: https://www.spitfireperformance.com/jl165.html (full page content)

Performance trials and brief handling assessment on a standard Spitfire IX with Merlin 66 adjusted for maximum boost of +25 lb/sq.in. 150 grade fuel to Specification RDE/F/253 used throughout. Engine conditions: 3000 rpm + 25 lb/sq.in. boost.

**Climbs:**  
- Maximum rate of climb in M.S. gear (radiator flaps open): 5080 ft/min up to 500 feet  
- Maximum rate of climb in F.S. gear (radiator flaps open): 4335 ft/min at 11 400 feet  
- Maximum rate of climb in F.S. gear (radiator flaps shut): 4750 ft/min at 11 400 feet  

Estimated increase in rate of climb below full throttle heights due to increase in boost from +18 to +25 lb/sq.in.: substantial (page notes ~950 ft/min and ~30 mph gains in related summaries).

**Comparison with BS.543 (rough):**  
| Condition | Radiators | JL.165 (estimated) | BS.543 |  
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|--------|  
| Max rate of climb in MS gear ft/min | Open | 4200 | 4700 |  
| Max rate of climb in FS gear ft/min | Open | 3500 | 3850 |  
| Max level speed in MS gear mph | Shut | 364 | 384 |  
| Max level speed in FS gear mph | Shut | 388 | 407 |  

Additional climb table points (page):  
- 0 ft: 4250 ft/min, 3000 rpm, +25, 170 ASI  
- 2000 ft: 4265 ft/min  
- 4000 ft: 4280 ft/min  
- 6000 ft: 4295 ft/min  
- 8000 ft: 4310 ft/min  
- 10 000 ft: 4325 ft/min  
- 11 400 ft: 4335 ft/min (F.S. open)

## 3. MA.648 and related notes

MA.648 (LF IX with different intake/prop, S.U. pump) is referenced in comparisons as having full throttle heights above average (by ~200 ft MS and ~800 ft FS relative to some serials). Specific full Table I not fully transcribed in the same public HTML format as JL.165, but used as a level-speed reference alongside BS.543 in later analyses.

**Instrument correction and run-mass notes:**  
- Level speed results typically corrected to 95% of take-off weight.  
- Climb results based on all-up weights.  
- Corrections per A&AEE/Res/170 or Memorandum 27.8.42 (position error, compressibility, temperature, altitude).  
- Mean flying weight for IX series around 7200–7450 lb depending on armament (2×20 mm + 4×.303 or 8×.303).
