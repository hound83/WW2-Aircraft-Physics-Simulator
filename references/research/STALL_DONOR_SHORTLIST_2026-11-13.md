# Independent clean-stall donor shortlist

13 November 2026. Fighter Sim Inputs v1.11.0. The comparison in [issue #95](https://github.com/hound83/WW2-Aircraft-Physics-Simulator/issues/95) asks Grok for an independent selection. The [weight-transfer audit](STALL_WEIGHT_TRANSFER_2026-11-13.md) records the aerodynamic grouping and numerical checks.

Use clean, power-off, quasi-steady 1g stall EAS with the donor's stall-run mass wherever possible. When wing area, effective maximum lift coefficient, CG/trim and external configuration are equivalent, scale by the square root of the target-to-donor mass ratio. Use the separate wing area term and reassess maximum lift for clipped tips, different wing roots or slats. Retain raw IAS and the source stall definition.

## Recommended order

1. **P-51B-15 and Spitfire LF IX** serve the existing benchmark. Get the Mustang's near-stall calibration and the Spitfire's exact tip/rear-fuselage and loading reference. The D/K handbook schedule is available now for within-family IAS checks.
2. **Bf 109 F-4/G-2** is the highest-leverage new transfer. Resolve the Soviet speed columns, get the stall-run mass, and use the RAF report as a separate check.
3. **P-47D-22/D-30** is the next practical cross-block test. Start with the D-30 trace and calibration. Match the D-22's power state and run mass before comparing.
4. **Fw 190 early A-series** needs the underlying British loading and instrumentation record. Keep A-8 and D-9 estimates specific to their configurations.
5. Check the conversion process against the exact **F4U-4 BuNo 80765** Navy reference, 89.4 kt CAS at 12,500 lb, clean power off. [BIS 2157 table](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/f4u/f4u.html)

Use these candidates for source recovery and provisional research. Promotion to a shared lift input requires both a defensible corrected anchor and the matching aerodynamic configuration.
