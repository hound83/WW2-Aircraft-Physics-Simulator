# Primary extraction batch list (ordered)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Purpose:** Logical, top-to-bottom batches of remaining primary-source extraction work.  
**Batch size:** ~5–8 items. Each batch is sized so it can be completed in one focused session.  
**Rule:** Prefer original tables over secondary summaries. Grade every number. Keep specimen, boost, cooling, mass, and intake explicit.
