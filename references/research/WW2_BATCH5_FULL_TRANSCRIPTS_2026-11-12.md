## 1. Focke-Wulf A-5 level-speed and climb tables (20 Oct 1943)

Source: https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/fw190a5.html (Focke-Wulf Flugzeugbau, Abt. Flugmechanik L, 20.10.43)

**Level speed (Normaljäger Fw 190 A-5, 1.42 ata / 2700 rpm):**  
- Sea level: 352 mph / 567 km/h  
- 20 669 ft / 6.3 km: 408 mph / 656 km/h  

**Climb:**  
- Sea level: 2 938 ft/min / 15.0 m/s  
- 2 953 ft / 0.9 km: 2 997 ft/min / 15.3 m/s  

Related sheets (same page):  
- Flugzeug-Entwicklungs-Blatt (1.11.44): 351 mph / 565 km/h SL, 407 mph / 655 km/h at 6.3 km; climb 3 290 ft/min / 16.8 m/s SL.  
- A-5/A-6 Handbuch (Dec 1943): 348 mph / 560 km/h SL, 410 mph / 660 km/h at 6.3 km.  

**Notes:** C-3 injection / erhöhte Notleistung trials (Aug–Sep 1943) showed higher speeds (up to ~578 km/h SL and 680 km/h at 5.2 km on one A-5), but these were not the standard operational rating.

## 2. WNr 783 Flugbericht Nr. 1 (open- vs closed-cooling)

Source: http://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/fw190-783-nr1.html (1 March 1943)

**Conditions:** BMW 801 D 2/20208, close engine cowling, split cooling cowl flaps, armament 2 MG 17 + 2 MG 151, take-off weight 3870 kg. Long or short exterior intake.

**Results:**  
- Airspeed indicator: no position error at 3 speeds on test course.  
- Level speed with long exterior intake, combat power, split flaps closed: Vwc = 405 mph / 652 km/h at CINA full throttle height 22 802 ft / 6950 m. Good agreement with earlier WNr 528 in low supercharger gear.  
- Climb with long intakes: service ceiling 34 941 ft / 10 650 m or 36 745 ft / 11 200 m at full throttle altitude 20 013 ft / 6100 m. Rate of climb in good agreement with WNr 528.  
- Pressure in front of and pressure drop in radial cylinder block documented for open (climb) and closed (level) flaps.  

**Notes:** Short intakes produced worse engine running; long intakes recommended for production. Climb properly flown only with long intakes.

## 3. Internal vs external intake speed curves

From WNr 783 and related reports:  
- Long exterior intake preferred; short exterior intake impaired engine performance.  
- Earlier WNr 528 (long exterior intakes) showed good agreement in low gear.  
- Internal intake loss of precompression noted as subject for further investigation.

## 4. Rechlin Vergleichsfliegen A-2 vs F-4 (quantitative)

Source: Already fully transcribed in Batch 2 (15023/41, 10 Dec 1941).  

Key points retained:  
- A-2 practically equal speed to F-4 at low/medium altitude; ~15–20 km/h slower at high altitude.  
- Climb strongly inferior (6 min slower from 1000–10 000 m).  
- Better high-speed control forces and roll rate.  

---

## 5. BMW 801 D power anchors

From public sources (BMW VB and related):  
- Standard Start- und Notleistung: 1.42 ata / 2700 rpm.  
- Climb & 30 min: 1.35 ata / 2450 rpm (~1595 PS low gear, ~1455 PS high gear).  
- Erhöhte Notleistung trials (1.58–1.65 ata): speed gains of ~36 km/h at 3 km and ~42 km/h at 5 km on test A-5; climb time reduction ~30% in one trial.  
- Cylinder and oil temperatures remained within limits in the documented boost-increase tests.
