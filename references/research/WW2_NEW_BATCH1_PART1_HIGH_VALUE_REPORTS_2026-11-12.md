## 1. NACA WR L-566 — Flying Qualities and Stalling Characteristics of XP-51

**Report:** NACA Wartime Report L-566 (originally Memorandum Report, April 1943).  
**Authors:** Maurice D. White, Herbert H. Hoover, Howard W. Garris.  
**Aircraft:** North American XP-51 (AAF No. 41-38).

**Publicly available content:**
- Requirements for satisfactory stalling characteristics were met in all respects **except for stall warning**.
- Short-period longitudinal oscillation disappeared within one cycle in all conditions tested.
- First airplane tested at Langley with a low-drag airfoil section.
- Full stall CAS/EAS by configuration, mass, and CG position exist in the 106-page report (PDF available on UNT / NTRS) but are not transcribed in open HTML.

**Status:** Qualitative stall behavior extracted; quantitative CAS/EAS tables remain in the PDF.

## 2. NACA WR L-86 / ACR L5H11a — Profile drag of P-47D

**Report:** NACA Wartime Report L-86 (originally Advance Confidential Report L5H11a, October 1945).  
**Author:** John A. Zalovcik.

**Publicly available quantitative content:**
- Minimum profile-drag coefficient **Cd = 0.0062** for the smooth section at 63% semi-span.
- Test range: Mach 0.25–0.69, lift coefficients 0.15–0.68, Reynolds numbers 7.7×10⁶ to 19.7×10⁶.
- Critical Mach number exceeded by at least 0.04 with no evidence of compressibility shocks (no increase in wake width or Cd).
- In slipstream, boundary-layer transition as far back as 20% chord on the upper surface at low lift coefficients.
- Related camouflage-paint report shows minimum Cd ≈ 0.0097.

**Status:** Key Cd values and test conditions extracted; full polar points (Cd vs CL and Mach) remain in the PDF figures.

## 3. NACA RM on P-47D-30 (AAF No. 43-3441)

**Report:** Flight measurements of flying qualities — longitudinal stability, control, and stalling characteristics.

**Publicly available content:**
- Mild buffeting approximately **4 mph above the stall** (power-on clean condition).
- Approximately **5 mph above the stall** in the glide condition.
- Initial tendency to roll right, then left; controllable with normal control use.
- Stalling characteristics considered satisfactory except in approach and wave-off conditions.
- Ample stall warning in the form of buffeting, increased stick forces, or rearward stick movement.
- Full time-history plots of stall approaches exist in the report.

**Status:** Quantitative warning margins and qualitative behavior extracted; full time-history data remain in the PDF.

## Full PDF transcriptions (available content)

### NACA WR L-86 / ACR L5H11a — complete public extraction

**Full title:** Flight Investigation of Boundary-Layer and Profile-Drag Characteristics of Smooth Wing Sections of a P-47D Airplane  
**Author:** John A. Zalovcik  
**Originally issued:** October 1945 as Advance Confidential Report L5H11a  

**Test setup:**
- Three wing sections measured (right wing 63% semispan, left wing 63% semispan, left wing 25% semispan in slipstream).
- Surfaces faired with glazing putty, sanded smooth, coated with white lacquer-based paint.
- Flight conditions: straight flight (level and dives) and steady turns at 12 000–24 000 ft; indicated airspeeds 155–530 mph; normal accelerations up to 5.2 g.
- Measurements: boundary-layer transition (total-pressure rakes), wake surveys (24-tube rake), pressure distributions.

**Profile-drag results (straight flight, right wing 63% semispan):**
- Cd ranged from 0.0075 (CL = 0.68) to 0.0062 (CL = 0.15).
- Minimum Cd = 0.0062 at CL = 0.15, R ≈ 7.7×10⁶, M ≈ 0.33.
- No effect from power variation (level flight vs. throttled slides).
- Mach variations up to 0.17 near critical Mach (~0.66–0.68) showed no compressibility effects (no wake-width increase or Cd rise).

**Turns:**
- Cd agreed with straight flight at CL > 0.40.
- At CL < 0.40, Cd slightly higher (up to 0.0066) due to a crack at 11.5% chord on the ammunition door.
- Minimum Cd = 0.0066 in second/third turn series.

**Boundary-layer transition:**
- In slipstream (25% semispan): laminar flow extended to 20% chord at low CL.
- Outside slipstream: transition farther aft.

**Critical Mach:**
- 63% semispan: Mcr = 0.66 (CL = 0.10) to 0.68 (CL = 0.80).
- 25% semispan: Mcr = 0.63 (CL = 0.10) to 0.67 (CL = 0.80).
- Aileron deflection (1.5–3.6° up) increased Mcr by ~0.015.

**Conclusions:**
1. Minimum Cd = 0.0062 (straight flight, CL = 0.15). No compressibility effects up to Mcr.
2. Laminar flow to 20% chord in slipstream at low CL.
3. Mcr = 0.66–0.68.
4. Surface crack increased Cd in turns at low CL.

### NACA RM on P-47D-30 (AAF No. 43-3441) — stall section extraction

**Stalling characteristics summary (from the report):**
- Stall approaches performed in all configurations, both with all controls used to hold wings level and with elevator alone (other controls fixed).
- **Power-on clean condition:** mild buffeting about 4 mph above the stall. Initial roll to the right followed by roll to the left. Controllable with normal control use (some difficulty). Strong buffeting during the stall. Warning and characteristics considered satisfactory.
- **Glide condition:** buffeting about 5 mph above the stall. Ample warning.
- **Approach condition:** mild buffeting about 3 mph above the stall. Aileron and rudder forces slightly high and irregular; maximum rudder deflection reached before the stall.
- Full time-history plots of stall approaches (Figs. 47–59) exist in the report, with recorded mass and CG (examples: ~12 400–12 770 lb, CG ~26.3–28.9% MAC).

**Note on L-566:** The XP-51 stall report PDF was not fully retrievable via public API in this pass. Qualitative findings (stall warning deficient) remain as previously noted. Full CAS/EAS tables stay inside the original 106-page document.

**Note on Spitfire PEC:** The position-error corrections section in AP 1565J is confirmed but the numerical table is not present in open HTML or partial extracts.
