# Batch 1 extraction: Bf 109 F-4 and G-1/G-2 (graded)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Batch:** 1 of the expanded extraction list  
**Scope:** Messerschmitt F-4 flown Datenblatt, Rechlin G-1 Kennblatt, calculated G-1 sheet, Finnish MT-215, Soviet notes.  
**Grade key:** observed = flight-test or official Kennblatt/Datenblatt; derived = calculated from observed with stated method; estimated = incomplete conditions.  
**Status:** First full pass on these sources. Matching specimen, boost, cooling, mass, and intake still required before simulator use.

- Service ceiling (Ga): 11.6 km

- Notes: Figures appear not fully corrected for compressibility (per Kurfürst commentary). Startleistung was blocked at the time of this sheet.

## Bf 109 G-1 Rechlin Kennblatt (Erprobungsnummer 1586, early 1943)
Source: E-Stelle Rechlin.  
Conditions: 1.3 ata / 2600 rpm. Fixed tailwheel (noted ~12 km/h speed loss).  
**Grade:** observed.

- Maximum speed: 650 km/h at 6400 m  
- Comparison notes (Kurfürst): Close to calculated 660 km/h at 7000 m after tailwheel correction.  
- Full climb table not fully transcribed in public sources; time to 5 km reported ~4 min 11 s in related Rechlin data.

## Soviet NII VVS notes (WNr 14513 and related)
Source: Soviet captured-aircraft trials (G-2 series).  
**Grade:** observed (configuration/mass focused).

- Gun-pod configuration increases mass significantly (exact delta varies by report).  
- Level speed and climb reported in good agreement with German sources when configuration-matched.  
- Full quantitative tables exist but require further page-level extraction from original Soviet reports.

## Contradictions and gaps
- F-4 660 km/h at 6.2 km (flown) vs calculated 635 km/h at 6 km — difference partly attributed to compressibility correction and specimen variation.  
- G-1 Rechlin 650 km/h at 6400 m (fixed tailwheel) vs calculated 660 km/h at 7000 m — consistent after ~12 km/h tailwheel correction.  
- Finnish MT-215 rated altitude lower than German average; supercharger or rpm variation likely.  
- Missing: full Rechlin G-1 climb table in public transcription, exact cooling state and instrument corrections for all, near-stall CAS/EAS.
