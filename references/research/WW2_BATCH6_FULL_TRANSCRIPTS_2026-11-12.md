# Batch 6 full transcripts (public sources)

**As of:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** Available public transcriptions of Fw 190 later marks (D-9 dry/MW50, A-6/A-8/A-9, D-11 notes).  
**Note:** Full original Flugbericht pages are on wwiiaircraftperformance.org; quantitative content is captured below. Dry vs MW 50 are kept separate where the sources distinguish them.

---

## 1. Fw 190 D-9 dry and MW 50 separate campaigns

Sources: https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/fw190d9test.html and German Flugberichte extracts.

**Dry / standard ratings (examples from D-9/210001 and 210002 series):**  
- Combat power (without ETC, movable wheel covers): ~641 km/h at 5900 m  
- Startleistung: ~665 km/h at 6150 m  
- With ETC 504 + fixed wheel covers: ~630 km/h at 5800 m (combat), ~655 km/h at 6050 m (start)  
- Removal of ETC + movable covers: +8 to 10 km/h across the speed range  
- Sea level examples: ~324–342 mph depending on configuration  

**MW 50 / boosted (separate):**  
- Speed gains from 1900 PS start increase: ~16–18 km/h below full throttle height  
- MW 50 (160 Ltr. nozzle): additional ~33–35 km/h  
- Published service figures: ~686 km/h at 6600 m with MW 50; climb ~21 m/s  
- Climb note: with flaps flush at 9000 m, +~2 m/s and ceiling to 10 500 m  

**Notes:** Engine sealing, ETC, and wheel covers each contribute measurable drag (~10–15 km/h combined in some analyses). Dry and MW 50 campaigns are not interchangeable; conditions must be matched.

## 2. Additional A-6 / A-8 / A-9 and D-11 Flugberichte quantitative tables

**A-8 (wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/fw190a8.html):**  
- 13.11.43 sheet: 557 km/h SL, 644 km/h at 6.3 km; climb 13.4 m/s SL  
- 12.1.44 / 1.10.44 / 25.10.44 sheets: 566–578 km/h SL, 652 km/h at 5.5 km  
- ETC 501 rack loss: ~12 km/h SL, ~15 km/h at 6 km  
- Boost increase trials (BMW VB): +36 km/h at 3 km and +42 km/h at 5 km (1.42 → 1.65 ata)  

**A-9:** Limited public full tables; BMW 801 TS / higher ratings referenced, but quantitative Flugberichte thinner than A-8.  

**D-11:** Referenced in Erprobungsbericht lists; quantitative public transcription limited in this pass.

## 3. Open-cooling area graded estimates

From A-series and D-9 reports:  
- Radiator/cowl flap position strongly affects climb (open for climb, closed/strak for level).  
- Direct propeller efficiency (η) measurements remain sparse; open-cooling drag is graded as estimated from flap-position comparisons rather than direct η.

## 4. Manufacturer useful-power or propeller measurements

Limited direct useful-power or propeller efficiency curves in the public Flugberichte for these marks. Power anchors remain engine ratings (Jumo 213A ~1750 PS dry / ~2100 PS with MW 50; BMW 801 D standard 1.42 ata).

## 5. Configuration and mass notes

- D-9 typical mass ~4300 kg in climb tests.  
- A-8 mass and armament vary with Rüstsatz; ETC and wheel covers are explicit drag items.  
- MW 50 tank (115 L) can alternatively hold fuel.
