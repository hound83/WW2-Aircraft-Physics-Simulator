# Stall-speed transfer between fighter variants

13 November 2026 · Research note for Fighter Sim Inputs v1.11.0

## When a sibling variant can supply the stall reference

For the same quasi-steady 1g stall condition, a donor stall in equivalent airspeed (EAS) transfers as

```text
Vs,target = Vs,donor × sqrt[(m_target / m_donor) ×
                          (S_donor / S_target) × (CLmax_donor / CLmax_target)]
```

If the effective wing area and maximum usable lift coefficient are shared, only the mass ratio remains. Internal ammunition, equipment, fuel or engine mass can be handled this way when their distribution leaves the center of gravity and trim comparable. A 10% mass increase raises stall EAS by 4.88%. More engine power by itself gives no weight-only correction in the clean power-off baseline. A changed propeller, slipstream or thrust component calls for a separate powered case. Confirm wingtip, slat/flap and wing-root geometry, external stores and ports, gear, radiator configuration, CG and the stall event definition before declaring the transfer applicable. [NASA lift equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/lift-equation/)

Prefer measured EAS or a source CAS that can be converted at the reported test condition. Where a documented correction covers the same installation, airspeed channel and near-stall range, it can qualify reported IAS for conversion. Otherwise an IAS ratio is a same-instrument handbook comparison or a diagnostic arithmetic check. Use actual stall-run loading when known and label handbook, trial gross and takeoff weights separately. A plausible transfer is a calculated input with its donor and assumptions; an independent matched target trial would test it.

## Which versions can share a donor

| Family | Strongest candidate transfer | Limits and present donor quality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fw 190** | **A-2/A-3/A-4** in matching clean radial-fighter configurations, especially A-3 → A-4 or armament/load differences within the same handbook configuration. The German handbook groups A-2/A-3 loading and documents the A-4 separately. | The captured A-3 has an approximate clean 110 mph and about 8,600 lb all-up elsewhere in Report 55. Exact stall mass, power and speed basis are missing. A-5/A-8 are a weaker extension until wing installation, armament openings, cooling and CG match. The D-9's Jumo installation, longer nose and redesigned tail warrant their own donor or explicit sensitivity. [Fw 190 A handbook](https://www.degnans.com/markd/Fw%20190%20A-2%20bis%20A-4%20Teil%2000.pdf) · [AFDU Report 55 §§11, 31](https://wwiiaircraftperformance.com/com/gb/documents/fw190a3_RAE-Test-Jul42-new.html) · [Smithsonian D-9](https://www.si.edu/object/nasm_A19600319000) |
| **Bf 109** | **F-1/F-2/F-4** in matched configuration, and **F-4 ↔ early G-2** as a provisional power-off transfer. RAF No. 209 Group describes the G-2 airframe as substantially similar to the F-4, with the new DB 605 A engine the major difference. Early G-2/G-4 changes can be screened in the same way. | Match slats and radiator/flaps, propeller state, armor, stores and CG. The E's different wing geometry warrants its own anchor. Later G variants need an installation check before transfer. F-4's 159 km/h is calculated, and the G-2/Trop's 112 mph is IAS without stall-run mass or stated power. Neither currently establishes a calibrated F/G donor. [US wartime Report 110, construction and dimensions](https://stephentaylorhistorian.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/bf-109f-evaluation.pdf) · [RAF G-2 technical trials §§2, 20](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/me109/me-109g2-trop.html) |
| **Spitfire** | **F IX ↔ LF IX ↔ HF IX** with the same tips, rear fuselage, wing installation and loading distribution. The Air Ministry describes essentially similar aircraft with different Merlin installations, and identifies the (e) armament change. A matched (e) configuration is a candidate where gun-port/wing details are equivalent for the test. | The pilot notes group stall by tip, load and rear fuselage. At the same loading class they report full-span speeds about 3–6 mph IAS lower than clipped. Clipping reduces wing area and can alter effective CLmax. Compare within tip type. Keep the early Mk I, Mk Va and Mk IX source rows separate until calibrated data supports a cross-mark transfer. [A.P. 1565J/P/L-P.N. §§1, 41–42, 53](https://zenoswarbirdvideos.com/Images/spit/SPIT9MANUAL.pdf) |
| **Mustang** | **P-51B ↔ P-51C** at matched block/installation, and **P-51D ↔ P-51K** as a shared clean IAS reference within the manual's weight range. The US Air Force museum identifies the B/C as nearly identical. AN 01-60JE-1 gives a common D/K clean table. | Match the propeller for powered cases. B/C-to-D/K requires separate evidence because the inboard leading-edge extension changes the effective wing-root profile. The P-51H has a different wing. Separate B-5/B-15 cockpit calibrations are also required before comparing their apparent stall differences. [USAF museum](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/196263/north-american-p-51d-mustang/) · [D/K manual p. 21](https://data.ntsb.gov/Docket/Document/docBLOB?FileExtension=.PDF&FileName=P51D+Normal+Operating+Instructions-Master.PDF&ID=40206631) · [Lednicer, wing geometry and Fig. 1](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/Lednicer_Fighter_Aerodynamics.pdf) |
| **P-47** | **Within matching P-47D installations**, fuel and internal equipment/loading are plausible weight-only changes. Across D blocks, keep the reference for the matched propeller and airframe equipment. **D-22 ↔ D-30** is a conditional candidate with clean gear/flaps, retracted dive-recovery flaps, comparable CG and an established airspeed basis. | The D-22 report gives raw ship IAS, and the D-30 NACA traces have a service-indicated channel and include a bubble canopy, round-nose ailerons, dorsal fin and recovery flaps. The B-to-D numbers do not support a weight-only transfer on the reported scales. The P-47N uses a different wing. [D-22 trial §§I, III, V.D](https://wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-47/p47-26167.html) · [NACA L8A06, description, Table I and Figs. 49–51](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20090022749/downloads/20090022749.pdf) · [USAF museum variant register](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/858869/republic-p-47/) |
