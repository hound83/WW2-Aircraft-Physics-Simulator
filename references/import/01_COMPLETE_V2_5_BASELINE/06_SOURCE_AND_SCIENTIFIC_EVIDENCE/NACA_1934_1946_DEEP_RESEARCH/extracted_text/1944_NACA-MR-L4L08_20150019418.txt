              At the request of the Army Air Forces, Air Technical
        Service Cormr.and, the NACA has conducted a series of tests
        to develop .wodifications that could improve the high-speed
        performance of the P-63A airplane. The program was carried
        out in three parts.
             Preliminary flight tests were made with the airplane
        in its original configuration to determine where-i,n possible

        ence 1.                                          ·

        would accompany minor design changes that could be made··

        the airplane was again flight tested, first in the original
        and then in a modif~:ed configuration incorporating a number

these tests included NACA pressure recorders for airspeed,
