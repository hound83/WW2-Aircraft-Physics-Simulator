      By means of a general theorem founded on the basis of
Prandtl~s   theory of the lifting line. a method is derived
for obtaining    the minimum induoed drag of airfoils     in the
proximity  of   ideal internal boundaries.    The theorem is
applied to the case of an ideal wing-fuselage       combination
consisting   of a lifting   line intersecting  an infinitely
long circular cylindrical     fuselage to determine   the effect
of wing height on the minimum induced drag.       The case of
ideal combinations    with constant circulation    is aleo con-
sidered in detail. ae it has been treated erroneously        ia a
previous analysis.     The analysis preeented   here incidental-

theory.
