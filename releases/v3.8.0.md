# v3.8.0 condition-aware flight envelope

Profile P adds a gear-specific fast-flight correction for the P-51B and Spitfire, source-bounded P-47B power at sea level and on the normal rating, and open-shutter configurations for the P-47B and Spitfire. Automatic gear selection uses net power at the flight state. The four original speed anchors and the established fixed-state climb checks serve as guardrails.

Level-speed mean absolute errors fall from 3.72 to 0.79 mph TAS for the Mustang and from 4.63 to 0.98 for the Spitfire. The P-47B's 0.81 mph speed error is preserved, and the main flight model now includes seven previously unavailable recorded rating/altitude states. The Bf 109 F-4's historical evidence gap prevents a supported 7,000 m power continuation. [Results, assumptions and held-out checks](../docs/FLIGHT_ENVELOPE.md)
