# v3.9.0 archival power and external flight checks

The working simulator offers an explicit, combat-only DB 601 E inlet-pressure transfer for the Bf 109 F-4 through 23,000 ft. It uses a historical G-1 test of the DB 601 E and F-series propeller as its full-throttle reference. At 7,000 m it estimates 633.2 km/h against the separate 645 km/h secondary F-4 figure. The result is a conditional estimate.

A separate production P-47B report checks level speed and cooling drag. The Spitfire and Mustang transfers retain their adverse results as evidence for keeping the fast-flight terms tied to the original specimens. [Source conditions, residuals and limitations](../docs/INDEPENDENT_FLIGHT_CHECKS.md)
