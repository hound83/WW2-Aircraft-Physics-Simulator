# v4.5 graph evidence and speed lock

Open the [operating benchmark](https://hound83.github.io/WW2-Aircraft-Physics-Simulator/#operating-benchmark).

- **Speed range** defaults to the first and last speeds where each aircraft can sustain 3°/s. That rate corresponds to a two-minute circle. Full envelope restores each aircraft’s stall and level-speed limits. Turn, turn advantage and climb share the same equivalent-airspeed axis. The probe moves in 5 mph EAS steps across the normal display span and converts the readout to the selected speed unit.
- **Colour** identifies the selected aircraft. Germany uses amber, the US blue, the UK teal and the USSR red. Two shades distinguish aircraft of the same country. The legend names both aircraft in comparison order.
- **Curve style** shows evidence coverage. Solid climb sections near the matched P-47B 41-5902 and Spitfire BS.543 schedules use the reported cooling and rating. Each altitude uses speeds bracketed by adjacent observations on the same engine branch. The values are calculated at the selected load. Dotted sections show model estimates, transfers or extrapolations. Sustained-turn curves currently use dotted lines. Source-constrained and provisional sections meet at the same calculated point.
- **Shading** shows the existing Mustang–Spitfire sensitivity bounds where both ends can be calculated. The centre line follows the model estimate. The readout flags a model value outside a supplied band. The bounds describe the tested assumptions and have no probability interpretation.
- **Lock speed** by clicking or tapping any of the three plots or using the button below the turn plot. The locked cards keep their source and related-calculation links available while you move the pointer. Click another point or move the speed slider to change the locked speed. Use **Unlock speed** or Escape to resume hover tracking.

The [11 November calculated-evidence pack](research/WW2_FIGHTER_CALCULATED_EVIDENCE_2026-11-11.md) supplies links to the available calculations for each variant. Its climb-time, lift, drag and other checks retain their original conditions. Coverage, plotted samples and tested ranges are separate inputs to the graph renderer. The flight model and its [four-aircraft scorecard](FLIGHT_SCORECARD.md) are the v4.4 baseline. The [v4.4 maneuver-lift and flight-test report](V44_TURN_PERFORMANCE.md) records the fit and held-out checks and remaining historical uncertainty.
