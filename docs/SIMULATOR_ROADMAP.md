# Simulator roadmap after 4.17

## Next milestone · M3 — Validate stall, acceleration and climb across all 14 aircraft

**Deliver one fleet update.** Start by comparing all 14 operating profiles at the current settings. Work through each performance metric across the roster, collect comparable tests together, and rank the discrepancies by how much they change the gap between aircraft. A chart-reading correction belongs to its source. A correction to wing, engine or propeller data belongs to every configuration the source covers. A change to a shared calculation gets tested on the full roster.

[4.17](V417_EVIDENCE_AND_PERFORMANCE.md) has numerical comparisons for time to altitude on 14 of 14 profiles, clean stall on 2 of 14 (both transferred from other aircraft), and level acceleration on 1 of 14 (a restored-aircraft proxy). **Correction and release rule.** Rank candidate changes by the number and importance of comparisons they improve. Check the source, units, configuration and test schedule first. Where a repeated error points to a shared calculation, fix that calculation once and rerun all 14. Where a source proves an input error, correct the affected variants together, preserving documented differences such as clipped wings, supercharger gear and radiator state. A single-aircraft transcription can be corrected in the same batch without extending it to relatives.

Publish all 42 aircraft/metric results, showing model values and identifying direct checks, transfers, proxies and missing evidence separately. Include signed before/after errors wherever measured, material regressions, orderings that change or remain uncertain, and the four existing reference speed/climb checks. Reserve observations before fitting and identify which come from another flight. Missing measurements remain explicit and do not hold up the fleet update. Seek original reports first, traceable secondary accounts next, and label IL-2 or DCS comparisons as simulator evidence.
