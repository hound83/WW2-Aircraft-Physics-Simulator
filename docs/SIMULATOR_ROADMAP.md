# Simulator roadmap after 4.17

**Next milestone: validate clean stall and timed level acceleration on the current roster, and locate the altitude bands responsible for the two largest early-aircraft climb errors.** The product decision is whether a pilot comparing two aircraft can trust the direction and size of the difference at the selected weight, altitude and power setting.

Milestones 1 and 2 shipped together in [4.17](V417_EVIDENCE_AND_PERFORMANCE.md). All 14 profiles have level-speed and time-to-altitude evidence groups. Eleven have linked clean-stall records, some from instruments without a low-speed correction or from sister aircraft. The three acceleration entries are a restored Mustang, Finnish timed segments and a derived Spitfire check. Five profiles have a roll record or a related-variant lead. These counts describe the available evidence. [Previous milestone plan](https://github.com/hound83/WW2-Aircraft-Physics-Simulator/blob/48e89ad0a629575c022ab4a7ac006553c91f23c3/docs/SIMULATOR_ROADMAP.md)

## Delivery order and decision to proceed

1. **Milestone 3 — clean stall, acceleration and a focused climb check.** Start with the first three rows as one release candidate. Keep the four reference aircraft's before/after speed and climb results beside every calibration change. Seek primary records first, then traceable secondary sources. IL-2 and DCS can provide labeled comparisons. If a target lacks a decisive measurement after its allotted search, record the missing datum and move on.
2. **Finish the comparison view and publish additional roll points as they become available.** These small additions can ship with milestone 3. A missing roll curve need not delay a supported stall or acceleration result.
3. **Milestone 4 — sustained level turn for one well-documented pair.** First establish the clean lift boundary and straight-flight power balance in the speed range of interest. Then compare a measured steady turn at matched speed, altitude, weight and rating. Expand to another family if the result holds on a separate check. A speed-losing half-turn answers a different question. [Existing maneuver evidence](V415_FLEET_MANEUVER_ENERGY.md)

**What can wait.** Add new aircraft when they fill a specific comparison gap. Revisit propeller efficiency, radiator drag or engine power when a matched test identifies which input matters. Full turn-to-recovery modeling needs a continuous timed record. Detailed stick-force or pilot-strength simulation, broader handling dynamics and fleet-wide tuning have lower priority than these measured comparisons.
