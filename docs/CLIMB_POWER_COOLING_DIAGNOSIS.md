| 75inHg | 3801.784 | -0.014 | 8660 | 680 | 4349.797 |

The 50 hp increment is an illustrative sign test, not measured cooling loss. The negative net CD0 required to improve these climbs cannot be implemented as additional positive cooling drag. It could algebraically represent less baseline drag or extra thrust; neither is established by this inversion. This does not rule out radiator heat recovery or exhaust effects.

0.83 is the mathematical amplitude of the current heuristic C formula, not a measured physical efficiency limit. Its constant-eta grid is a climb-only envelope at the frozen polar; it is not an anchor-preserving replacement. Mass roots are shown to the nearest 10 lb and are unverified counterfactuals, not inferred fuel burn. All calculations retain the original reference polar rather than refitting drag to climb.
