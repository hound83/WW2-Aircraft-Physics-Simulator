## Bf 109 F-4

- Messerschmitt's **29 November 1941 flown-data sheet** gives 2,890 kg in the flight loading block, a separate **135 km/h at 2,560 kg** in the landing block, and a five-point climb-time table. The landing fuel allowance is 15 minutes at continuous power. Power during the landing, flap angle, speed correction and stall criterion are unspecified. The **1 July 1942 calculated sheet** separately reports the same landing speed at a slightly different loading. Both scans are linked to the earlier transcribed data so repeated figures count once. [November flown sheet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141110152256im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B015.jpg) · [July calculated sheet](https://web.archive.org/web/20160323022701im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B016.jpg)
- **Rechlin 15023/41** is a separate December comparison between production F-4 and A-2 aircraft with full operational load and ammunition. The F-4 took **4:00 from 1,000 to 5,000 m** and **12:30 from 1,000 to 10,000 m**. The climb settings are unspecified. The comparison also records separation after three minutes at combat power, an approximate 15 km/h landing-speed advantage for the F-4 and inconclusive minimum turn-radius results. [Scope and load](https://web.archive.org/web/20160323022642im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B008.jpg) · [Combat power comparison](https://web.archive.org/web/20160323022642im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B009.jpg) · [Climb and handling](https://web.archive.org/web/20160323022642im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B010.jpg)
- The **3 February 1943 factory sheet** prints a landing lift coefficient of **1.70** and a high-speed drag coefficient of **0.023**. Use these as period design references with an unspecified measurement method. A conditional level-lift calculation is filed with its assumptions. The WTS Rechlin curve and OKL compilation support a period emergency-speed reference around 670 km/h, with shared original lineage possible. The February 1942 installation manual confirms a **1.685:1** engine-to-propeller reduction for DB 601 E and distinguishes it from DB 601 N. [Factory sheet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509054935im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B017.jpg) · [WTS curve](https://web.archive.org/web/20110718202410im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B001.jpg) · [OKL table](https://web.archive.org/web/20110718202435im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B014.jpg) · [Installation manual p. 607](https://web.archive.org/web/20110718202553im_/http://www.beim-zeugmeister.de/zeugmeister/uploads/pics/_LW_A0001_B013.jpg)

## Bf 109 G-2

- The NII VVS handling trials on **WNr 14513** use **3,235 kg at takeoff** for the control-input and speed-loss flights, with CG set to 25.0% or 30.7% MAC. The report gives separate weights for the airframe, payload and 29 kg of instruments. It records flaps-up slat opening at **235 km/h IAS right and 225 left**, and closing at 250 right and 260 left. Photo 21 supplies a glide speed and altitude trace. Keep the p. 43 minima, Photo 21's unqualified speed axis, and the mass at actual break distinct. The instrumented aircraft's heavy loading limits transfer to a clean service G-2. [NII VVS original pp. 10, 33, 43–45](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/me109/g/g_files/Me-109_G2_14513_Russian.pdf)
- The **June 1943 pilot instructions** allow **1.30 ata / 2,600 rpm for 30 minutes** and block the listed **1.42 ata / 2,800 rpm** emergency setting. The original June 1942 copy also blocks that setting and contains a handwritten best-climb indication change from 250 to 270 km/h near the ground. The Soviet full-throttle trim chart specifically records **2,600 rpm and 1.3 kg/cm² as printed**. Its setting applies to that trim run. [June 1943 pilot instructions](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/me109/g/g_files/BF109_G-2-4-6_Bedienungsvorschrift_June43.pdf) · [June 1942 pilot instructions](https://stephentaylorhistorian.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/bf-109g-2.pdf) · [NII VVS Photo 5](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/me109/g/g_files/Me-109_G2_14513_Russian.pdf)
- Messerschmitt **109 11 L 42** adds nine timed-course rows across eight production G-2s. The 1.30 kg/cm² boost and 1.80 kg/cm² blower figures are the standardization settings. The report estimates **7 km/h** tolerance in the absolute speeds. The printed engine type on Blatt 2 is overwritten and exact test RPM is unverified, so those rows support a relative surface-treatment comparison. The separate Vorflügel report shows that rigging can shift slat opening materially, even within the early G family. [WNF speed test](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/me109/g/g_files/VB-109-11-L-42.pdf) · [Factory slat trials](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/me109/g/g_files/VB-109-10-E-42.pdf)

## Fw 190 A-4 and D-9

- The A-4 handbook specifies flight loadings of **3,862, 3,985, 4,176 and 4,411 kg** for four armament and stores configurations. WNr **783** was tested at **3,870 kg** and **2,400 rpm** with a BMW 801 D-2 and long external intakes. Ten points cover the factory climb comparison with cooling flaps open and shut. At 5,000 m the graph shows approximately **12.1 and 13.1 m/s**, respectively. [A-4 handbook p. 12](https://www.degnans.com/markd/Fw%20190%20A-2%20bis%20A-4%20Teil%2000.pdf) · [WNr 783 Nr. 1 chart 2](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/fw190/a4/a4_files/Fw_190_783_Nr1.pdf)
- D-9 WNr **210002** chart 2 lists a **4,100 kg** start weight for level flight. Chart 3 lists **4,300 kg** for the **3,000 rpm** combat climb with ETC 504, ammunition and a full range fuel tank. Nr. 1 also gives a bounded Eltron correction at **360–550 km/h** with two compressibility treatments. Nr. 3 shows **13–15 km/h** from temporary gap sealing at two ratings before the seals failed. Rechlin WNr **210006** adds open-radiator curves at 3,000 and 3,250 rpm with a separately documented configuration. [WNr 210002 Nr. 1](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/fw190/d9/d9_files/Fw_190_D-9_210002_FB_Nr1.pdf) · [Nr. 3](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/fw190/d9/d9_files/Fw_190_D-9_210002_FB_Nr3.pdf) · [Rechlin Erpr.Nr. 9003](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/fw190/d9/d9_files/Fw_190_D-9_006.pdf)
- Yakubovich's NII VVS table gives a captured A-4 **23–24 s at 1,000 m flaps up**, **22–23 s at 10°**, and **154 km/h landing speed** with no event mass given. It gives D-9 **WNr 210251** **22–23 s at 1,000 m** and **27–28 s at 5,000 m**. These are published turn durations for separate specimens. Test speed, boost and energy retention remain unverified, so the rows belong to augmented coverage. Sobolev and Khazanov trace the D-9 campaign to RGAE f. 8164, op. 1, d. 214, ll. 7–7 verso and report that water-methanol emergency power was untested. [Yakubovich Table 3](https://ru.djvu.online/file/hR5Asibxj3nWb) · [Sobolev and Khazanov pp. 210–211 and note 71](https://djvu.online/file/A3MamKLu2C42j)
