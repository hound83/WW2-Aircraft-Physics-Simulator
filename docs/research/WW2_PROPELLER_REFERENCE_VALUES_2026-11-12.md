# Fighter Sim Inputs v1.3.0 · Propeller reference values

**80% is the common starting value. 76% is the Curtiss Thunderbolt value under the military climb and high-speed conditions below.** Greg's Mustang workbook uses a constant 80% and one reference drag coefficient. This study uses that same power balance and checks the numbers against wartime flight data. ## What the propeller measurements support

The pack adds **11 Curtiss high-speed runs** from L4L07 Table II. Each indexed row agrees with the printed thrust and power coefficients and the helical tip Mach within the reading precision. Across **103 direct flight readings from two propeller installations**, the 80% constant has **2.16 percentage points** mean absolute error. The current momentum and loss equation, evaluated at the same advance ratio, loading and tip Mach, has **8.15 points**. At the seven sampled fast-flight points with advance ratio 1.55–2.15 and tip Mach 0.94–1.02, the observed mean is **80.64%**. At the seven fast-flight points above tip Mach 1.02, the observed values span **66.4–81.9%**. [NACA L4L07](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930093302) · [NACA L5G09a](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930093653) · [New original table readings](../../references/research/ww2_propeller_efficiency_2026-11-11.json)

The seven-aircraft check fixes shaft power, mass and induced drag inputs for each report condition and sets one level drag coefficient from its speed anchor. On the full test ranges, the flat-80% speed errors were **6.01 mph** for BS.543, **7.30 mph** for the P-51B, **10.28 mph** for the P-38G and **22.47 mph** for the Fw 190 A-4. These are wider than the respective current model errors of **0.98, 0.79, 4.99 and 6.83 mph**. The F-4 has one secondary speed check at **2.94 mph** error. The low-blower recommendations are scoped to the rows above. The BS.543 open-radiator fit with 80% and the current chart power would require a negative added area at its climb anchor. That result supports using the recorded climb points for that branch. [Replay details for all seven fighters](../../references/analysis/fighter-propeller-efficiency-v1.json)

## Using the pack

Build output includes `dist/fighter-propeller-efficiency.json`. Read `working_reference.reference_values` for the two numbers, `working_reference.level` for the drag references and speed checks, and `working_reference.climb` for the fixed-area climb replay. `working_reference.p38g_part_power` carries both historical power schedules and the interpolation checks. The efficiencies are calculated reference inputs for the stated conditions. The original flight readings stay linked to their reports. [Simulator handoff](../SIMULATOR_DATA_HANDOFF.md)
