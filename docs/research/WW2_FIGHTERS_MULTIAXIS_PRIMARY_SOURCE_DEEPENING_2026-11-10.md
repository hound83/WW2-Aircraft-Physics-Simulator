## Track 1: exact low-speed IAS, installation corrections, CAS/EAS

### P-38H/J/L: original 17-anchor correction and conditional *corrected* estimates

The [original P-38H/J/L/F-5B pilot handbook](https://www.airwar.ru/other/bibl/p-38pilot.pdf), printed p.33 / PDF p.37, gives **14 clean** and **3 gear/flaps-down** paired cockpit IAS versus historically called *Calibrated\** IAS values. Its footnote specifically says **installation errors only** and excludes instrument errors established by the Field Test Set. Consequently these are **installation-corrected IAS\***, *not proven full CAS*. Correction signs change across the speed range: at 83mph cockpit clean IAS the counterpart is 100mph, at 200mph they coincide, at 377mph cockpit IAS the counterpart is 360mph.

We do not squander a documented correction: for three existing handbook **clean 1g stall IAS** observations the position/installation part can be interpolated **inside** the original IAS 83–106mph interval, producing source-dependent candidates:

| Handbook gross weight | Original clean stall IAS | Installation-corrected\* candidate | Final verified CAS? |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 15,000 lb | 94 mph | **109.57 mph** | No: instrument error unknown |
| 17,000 lb | 100 mph | **114.78 mph** | No: instrument error unknown |
| 19,000 lb | 105 mph | **119.13 mph** | No: instrument error unknown |

These are *derived companion rows*, not three new measured stalls and not silently reinserted in the model's CAS or CLmax fitting. Landing-stall IAS 69–78mph is **below** the separate landing calibration's minimum 89mph and is left entirely uncorrected. Moreover the handbook's P-38H/J/L series does not cover the modeled P-38G. Locate the original P-38D–G T.O. 01-75F-1 with tabulated instrument/installation corrections; the [archive lead](https://www.xtrmntr.com/lightning/repo.html) is not yet an audited numerical source.

### Spitfire IX: a *documented*, but speed-limited, inter-airframe correction

The original [A&AEE BS.543 LF Mk IX performance report](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/spitcom/mk9/spitLF-HF-mkIX.html), section 4, explicitly says it **adopted the position-error correction measured on BF.274**, whose pressure head make, position and setting matched BS.543. This is legitimate original method transfer rather than an arbitrary other-airframe analogy. The [BF.274 report](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/spitcom/mk9/spitFmkIX_BF274.html) measured its original position error at IAS **130–320mph**. Ten *applied* PEC values are retained, including 160mph +2 and 136mph +4; 127mph +4.8, 121mph +5.3, 115mph +5.7 and 109mph +6.2 were **applied by the original authors below the measured interval**. We preserve the distinction between measured graph and historical author extrapolation. A 75–90mph Spitfire IX stall is not retroactively CAS.

### Further corrected and direct CAS leads

**10 Nov source erratum:** the initial assertion below that the cited Ki-84 T-2 report contains a p.8 calibration *graphic* was retracted after inspecting the full original. Page 8 contains a textual original **IAS stall** account, **not** a calibration chart. No Ki-84 CAS can be calculated from that report. See [the twelve-fighter source audit](WW2_TWELVE_FIGHTER_SUPERGOODENOUGH_GAP_AUDIT_2026-11-10.md) and version 1.0.1 of the underlying low-speed pack. The original Ki-84 report also confirms **no actual performance climbs were flown**.

- The [matched P-51B-15 43-24777 Eng-47-1749-A](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/p51b-24777.html) has nine original **IAS** stall rows at exact individual test masses (already registered before this release) and [its own original calibration Fig.1](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/P-51B_24777_Airspeed_Calibration.jpg). The relevant figure has **not been validated numerically at 67–86mph IAS**; no corrected CAS is invented or borrowed from the different P-51B-5/V-1650-3.
- Original [F6F-3 BuAer 04776 NACA Part I](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930092600/downloads/19930092600.pdf) calibrated a *position-error-free service IAS* through low-speed trailing-bomb formation methods. That improves interpretation of the Part III [stall peak normal-force coefficients](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930092602/downloads/19930092602.pdf), but instrument and compressibility corrections still need tracing before the result is certified **CAS**.
- The exact [Fw 190 D-9 WNr210002 German original Flugbericht Nr.1](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/Fw_190_D-9_210002_FB_Nr1.pdf) has a *Fahrtmesserkorrektur* graph, with/without compressibility, PDF p.4. It is an excellent future digitization target, but its validated IAS interval near the stall is **not established**. The [Ki-84 captured T-2 original report](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/japan/T-2_Report_on_Frank_I_Ki-84.pdf) instead provides **three genuine original IAS stalls** and **no calibration graphic**; its original calibration remains to be located in another source.
- The original [Fw 190 A-4 WNr783 report](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/fw190/a4/fw190-783-nr1.html) says its pitot installation showed no position error at **three unspecified check speeds**. Three high-speed checks cannot establish zero **low-speed** error at stall. The A6M3 EB-201 original correction starts **120mph IAS** while stall IAS is 72–76mph; **no stall-CAS extrapolation**.
- Four original **direct-CAS** F4U-4 BuNo80765 stall points remain registered independently of this extension (the 4th condition label is repeated ambiguously in the original). Direct **CAS/EAS hunting continues alongside correction-table hunting**, not displaced by these intermediate corrected\* results.

## Track 2: stall and turning/lift, with independent weight and method

- [F6F-3 BuNo 25820 original 1943 AAF trial](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/f6f/f6f-3-25820.pdf): **four newly added original stall IAS** at 65kt clean/off, 60kt clean/on, 52kt landing/off and 45kt landing/on; recorded also as correctly converted mph. **Weight not given**, and knot IAS is not CAS.
- The [P-38G versus F 1943 tactical report](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-38/p-38g-tactical-trials.html) reports approximately 110mph versus 145mph **turn-stall IAS** with maneuver flaps at 25,000ft. These are turning-stall observations, **not 1g stall or maximum sustained-turn rate**, and exact G/weights/airframes are unknown.
- [NACA P-47D-30 43-3441](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20090022749/downloads/20090022749.pdf) has steady-turn **elevator stick-force gradients**, e.g. approximately 7.5lb/g at 200mph and 11lb/g at 350mph under forward CG. These are **control feel**, not sustained turn deg/s. Stall warning buffet occurs approximately 3–5mph before fully developed stall in specified configurations. Figures 56–57 never complete a stall; figures 58–59 are transient accelerated stalls, not sustained performance.
- The same P-47D document has a **source contradiction** we corrected in a previous research pack: Figure 58's index says **3,000ft**, while its plotted title says **5,000ft**. Prior archive silently used 3,000ft; revised v1.0.1 stores altitude `null` and both competing original labels. No false precision.
- The [Spitfire VA original NACA companion](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930092582) identifies test serial **W3119** and confirms 6,184lb, 31.4%MAC. An R7347 photograph in related materials **does not prove** that R7347 was the measured airplane; previous metadata corrected. The cockpit IAS was reported roughly **10mph low near minimum**, but the stall table's NACA recorder signal cannot be assumed the same as the pilot meter. No arbitrary +10 correction is applied.
- [NACA F6F-3 04776 Part I](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930092600/downloads/19930092600.pdf) additionally yields 10,600lb/24.4%MAC, 11,303lb/28.5%MAC, and 11,500lb/31.2%MAC test-loading pairs. These are **Part I takeoff configurations**, not the Part III *instantaneous* stall-run mass (approximately 11,200lb adjusted for burned fuel).
- Secondary cross-checks, clearly not source-direct CAS: Bf109 G-2/Trop 112mph clean /102mph landing IAS ([contemporary 209 Group evaluation](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/me109/me-109g2-trop.html), no verified weight/serial); Tempest V JN731 85mph clean /74mph landing IAS at 11,480lb ([secondary book attribution](https://erenow.org/ww/flying-limit-testing-world-war-ii-single-engined-fighter-aircraft/7.php), original A&AEE scan absent). These provide *search leads*, not fit-ready aerodynamic truth.

**Key limitation:** no newly discovered primary report directly measures a full **maximum sustained turn-rate versus speed versus altitude versus gross mass** envelope. Steady-turn stick forces and *accelerated* stalls must not be misrepresented as those data. These observations can help validate CLmax/controls **when their conditions are known**; energy maneuverability remains conditioned on propulsion and aerodynamic drag.

## Track 3: primary engine, propeller, cooling, mass and power chain

**Best controlled propeller comparison: original USAAF [P-47B AAF 41-5942, 26 Dec 1942](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-47/p-47_files/P-47B_41-5942_FS-M-19-1523-A.pdf) and [28 Jan 1943](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-47/p-47_files/P-47B_41-5942_FS-M-19-1533-A.pdf).** Same aircraft, Pratt & Whitney R-2800-21 with **torque meter**, 12,620lb tested, six cuffed/uncuffed and additional prop designs. **68 published speed/climb scalar rows**: 24 level TAS, 44 ROC, each paired with actual prop name, heights, power and source configuration. December uses 30.32%MAC, January 28.3%MAC and *uncuffed* props. The Hamilton Standard 6501A-0 reaches 2,700fpm at 10,000ft vs 2,420fpm for cuffed Curtiss 714 at same torque power, but those are not aerodynamic drag-only effects. January climbrate table prints **IAS** whereas footnote says its speeds are the SAME as the December **TAS** speeds: airspeed basis marked `unestablished` instead of pretending this document is internally consistent. Cooling restrictions also make these **thermally usable** climb speeds, not guaranteed aerodynamic best ROC speeds.

**[P-40F AAF 41-13635](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-40/p-40_files/P-40F_41-13635_FS-M-19-1549-A.pdf)** adds **25** exact 5-prop speed/climb records, 18,000–28,000ft, V-1650-1, clean gear/flaps but with empty belly shackles. The original source contradicts itself over blade designation **89303-23W** versus **89303-24W** and spinner **61401.5-21** versus **6141C1.5-21**: preserved exactly, not joined by guesswork. [P-40B 41-5205](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-40/p-40_files/P-40B_41-5205_PHQ-M-19-1227-A.pdf) adds ten climb observations including service/absolute ceiling with its original V-1710-33 and 6,835lb weight.

**[P-51B-15 43-24777](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/p51b-24777.html)** adds original 75-versus-67inHg same-airframe speed, rack and climb comparators. Original critical 20,800ft: 75inHg with racks 431mph TAS, same power *without* racks 444mph, and 67inHg with racks 417mph. The report clearly says these **BHP numbers come from a Packard motor manufacturer altitude chart**, not measured propeller shaft torque. 44-1 fuel tests did **not** fly a separate ordinary-fuel baseline on that specimen. This exact **B-15/V-1650-7** must not be mixed with B-5/V-1650-3 instrument-corrected stall evidence.

**[F4U-4 BuNo80765 Navy inspection trial](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/f4u/f4u-4-80765.pdf)** adds 8 speed and 8 ROC original anchors in *normal* versus *military* settings (original speed in knots). Critical heights were partly extrapolated by the original authors; per-point direct-versus-extrapolated still needs exact original row qualification. Same original report already supplies the four CAS stalls, so these are linked as one test, not scored as independent validation.

**[Fw 190 D-9 WNr210002](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/Fw_190_D-9_210002_FB_Nr1.pdf)** adds 8 original combat/takeoff speed anchors with and without ETC504 and different wheel doors, [4 increased boost/MW50 anchors](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/Fw_190_D-9_210002_FB_Nr2.pdf) and [one approximate +9mph seal-gain experiment](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/Fw_190_D-9_210002_FB_Nr3.pdf). The 4,300kg quoted weight is explicitly associated with the **climb** graph, not automatically with every speed test. Nr1's full 4,300kg climb chart and speed-altitude curves are **identified but not yet point-digitized**. Weather, power and doors must be controlled before applying aerodynamic differences.

**[Bf109 G-1 WNr14026 original DB601E versus DB605A report](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/me109/VB-109-19-L-42.pdf)** adds 4 sea-level/critical-altitude performance pairs, but **both the engine and propeller** were changed between tests. It cannot quantify a pure DB605A engine gain and certainly cannot substitute for modeled **Bf109 F-4** data. Original [NACA P-47D propeller research](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20050019331/downloads/20050019331.pdf) separately records 3–4% efficiency decreases at 165mph IAS when switching between high/low power conditions: relevant propeller diagnostic, NOT a universal corrected efficiency map.

## Track 4: variant expansion, balanced coverage and readiness

**Readiness test:** *super good enough* requires at minimum source-audited, correctly scaled near-stall speed or CLmax **at meaningful flight weight(s)**, identifiable propulsion and cooling, independent measured speed and climb checkpoints, and a sufficiently constrained **sustained turn energy/drag envelope** with explicit uncertainty. Many separate table rows from one trial or approximate IAS do not satisfy this by count alone. The physics workstream retains ownership of implementing/verifying any solver correction and 5:1 test.

| Family/actual variants | Most valuable *current* evidence | Still blocking *super good enough* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P-51B-15 43-24777** (modeled) | Nine exact-stall-weight IAS rows, original airspeed-calibration Fig.1 lead, boost/rack controlled tests, V-1650-7/prop identified | Original Fig.1 **verified at 67–86 IAS**, installed prop thrust vs speed, flight mass in climbing/turning, direct turn-drag or independent sustained rate. |
| **P-47B 41-5902** (modeled), plus **41-5942** trials | Precise original 41-5902 speed/climb; 68 extra **different** 41-5942 prop comparison entries, torque BHP | 41-5902 stall-domain CAS or matched instrument+position PEC; 41-5942 cannot serve as same-airframe stall calibration; low-speed prop efficiency/turn drag. |
| **Spitfire LF IX BS.543** (modeled) | A&AEE speed/climb, tested mass, originally authorized BF.274 position PEC | BF.274 **below 130mph** independently measured or same-serial low-speed PEC, direct weight/configuration stall, independent turn/engine validation. |
| **Bf109 F-4** (modeled) | Original speed/engine history, related G-1 exact engine/prop comparison | Original **F-4 same-condition** stall IAS→CAS, weight and *F-series* DB601E prop installation data; G-1 data cannot silently transfer. |
| **Fw190 A-4 WNr783** (modeled) | Original speed/climb and 3-speed pitot no-error check | Measured low-speed PEC/1g stall at test weight, verified turn CLmax and speed/prop/drag conditions. D-9 is a **different wing/engine/variant**. |
| **P-38G-1 42-12687** (modeled) | Original clean speed/climb, engine-chart power; separate F/G tactical *turn-stall* context | Exact G-model original IAS/CAS chart, direct 1g stall at mass and G-variant prop/cooling; H/J/L handbook **not same variant**. |
| **F4U-4 80765** (candidate) | **4 direct-CAS stalls at 12,500lb**, 16 complementary same-airframe normal/military speed/climb anchors | Direct turn-drag/power and multiple stall masses; original 4th stall power label inconsistency; 1948 test temporal scope. **Best new intake candidate**, not fully cleared. |
| **F6F-3 04776 / 25820** (candidate) | NACA fuel-adjusted CN vs power/config, instrument-calibration method; separate 25820 IAS stalls; Navy multi-serial stall vs mass | Direct same-trial CAS/EAS/IAS trace, known stall instantaneous weight, independent sustained turn and power/prop installation. |
| **P-47D-30 43-3441** (candidate) | Test mass/CG across stall approaches, measured lb/g in steady turns and approximate buffet margins | Numeric 1g CAS and maximal sustainable rate; no direct ROC/drag from stick force. Fig.58 altitude source conflict. |
| **P-40F 41-13635 / P-40B 41-5205** (candidate) | 25/10 original propeller, climb and altitude results | Same variants original near-stall speed plus calibrated installation, stall weights/CLmax, realistic turn power. |
| **Fw190 D-9 210002** (candidate) | Thirteen original special-configuration level/speed-change records, original calibration and ROC graphs | Digitize original low-speed calibration range, 4300kg climb, exact speeds/flight weights and direct stall/turn. |
| **Bf109 G-1 14026 / G-2 Trop** (candidate) | Original mixed engine/prop performance, G-2 209 Group approximate IAS stalls | Variant-specific originals and masses, corrected IAS/CAS, disentangle prop/engine, turn/drag evidence. |
| **Spitfire VA W3119** (research reference) | Ten original IAS/CLmax pairs at 6184lb/31.4%MAC, separate cockpit IAS correction | NACA stall recorder channel vs pilot-meter mapping; not a proxy for LF Mk IX. |
| **A6M3 Model32 EB-201 / Ki-84 T-2 / Tempest V JN731** (research intake) | Original A6M3 high-speed correction and IAS stall; Ki-84 original chart; Tempest book-attributed IAS | Low-speed correction interval, exact original scans/test mass, engine performance, independent turn/drag. Tempest original A&AEE report still absent. |

**Verdict:** **0 of the 6 modeled variants have *full historical stall + sustained-turn + independent power* super-good-enough evidence yet.** F4U-4 is currently the strongest candidate *for direct stall-CAS quality*, but does not yet have a proven complete turn-energy envelope. This is an evidence-readiness verdict, not a claim that existing physics outputs are wrong.

## Highest-priority continuing source hunts (uninterrupted research branches)

1. **Direct source-matched low-speed CAS/EAS** or full instrument+installation error tables at **P-51B-15 43-24777, P-47B 41-5902, Spitfire BS543/F4 and P-38G 42-12687** stall speeds, weight and gear/flap/power matched. ### Audit and deployment contract

`npm run check` must pass the existing physics baseline and new source-specific tests. The source explorer automatically includes both compact JSON packs, source links, caveats and lineage. The original IAS→CAS gate remains fixed at the previous **4 direct CAS + 1 provisional CAS\***; we did **not** turn plausible corrections into artificially certain new CAS values. A physics workstream consuming this pack must explicitly declare each accepted report/variant, calibrated basis, instrument flags, speed/mass/altitude bounds, source dependence and validation holdouts before a new simulator version claims improved aerodynamic accuracy.
