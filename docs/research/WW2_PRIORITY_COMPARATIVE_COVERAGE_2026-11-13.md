## Evidence decisions

- **Fw 190 A-8 WNr 733705.** Flight Report Nr. 1 adds four corrected level-speed points from the earlier engine and two idle nose-drop readings from the Panzerblitz mock-up investigation. The low-speed values are 190–200 km/h with flaps and gear up and 172 km/h with them down. Exact stores state and low-speed calibration remain open. The captured A-3 stall stays with AFDU Report 55. The factory A-8 speed summaries remain on their separate dates and ratings. The Nr. 2 archive OCR and the English transcription give different combat full-throttle altitudes for the 589 km/h reading; recheck the scan before selecting the altitude. [Nr. 1](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/fw190/fw190a8-733705-1.html) · [AFDU Report 55](https://wwiiaircraftperformance.com/com/gb/documents/fw190a3_RAE-Test-Jul42-new.html)
- **RAF comparisons.** Retained the A-3 versus Spitfire Vb relative speed and climb readings as nine tactical comparison records. The +16 lb Vb comparison was cancelled. The Mustang III FZ.107 tests use a V-1650-3 and their own Spitfire IX and XIV comparators. Spitfire Va W.3119 stall evidence remains separate from X.4922. [AFDU Report 55](https://wwiiaircraftperformance.com/com/gb/documents/fw190a3_RAE-Test-Jul42-new.html) · [AFDU Mustang III](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/mustang/mustang-tactical.html) · [NACA L-353](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930092581)
- **P-47D-22-RE 42-26167.** Captured the 13,230 lb takeoff condition and the level/climb cooling differences. The ship's raw 116 mph idle IAS with gear and flaps up comes from the 44-1 fuel trial. Actual stall-run mass and applicable correction remain open. [Wright Field Eng-47-1774-A §§III and V](https://wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-47/p47-26167.html)
- **P-51D-15-NA 44-15342.** Captured the Hamilton Standard K6523A-24 assembly drawing and bounded the single-anchor sea-level drag polar using explicit efficiency and Oswald-efficiency assumptions. The central CD0 is 0.01453 and the combined sensitivity scenarios give 0.01344–0.01560. The 9,700 lb run point includes wing racks. Use Figure 1 to verify the low-speed calibration envelope and Figures 4/8 to test power required and drag at a lower speed. [Wright Field TSCEP5E-1908 §§III–IV](https://wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/p-51/p51d-15342.html)
- **Bf 109 E.** Reused the WNr 1304 glide speeds and cataloged the published approximate 890 ft full-throttle level turn radius at 12,000 ft as secondary context. Speed, bank and a timed trial record remain open. V15a and WNr 1792 speed evidence have separate airframe identities. [Morgan and Smelt reproduction](https://www.pilotfriend.com/flight_reports/reports/33.htm) · [E-series flight reports](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.org/me109/me109e.html)
- **Bf 109 G-2 and Fw 190 D-9.** The composite 535/660 km/h and 5.7 min G-2 claims still need exact source, installation and rating attribution. The D-9 Nr. 4 curve is tied to the sealed, polished, methanol-equipped WNr 210002 and a restricted low-gear test, with calculated curves identified by the report. The 606/650 km/h summary remains context. [G-2 evidence register](WW2_GERMAN_FIGHTER_ARCHIVE_2026-11-11.md) · [D-9 Flugbericht Nr. 4](https://www.wwiiaircraftperformance.com/org/fw190/d9/Fw190D-9_210002_Nr4.html)
