The lift calculation needs equivalent airspeed (EAS). Cockpit IAS first needs the correct instrument and installation corrections to obtain CAS. TAS then depends on altitude. Published indications and model predictions are shown separately below.

P-51B: calibrated lift candidate

NACA TN1044 used position-calibrated probes in gradual stalls with engine throttled and propeller in high pitch. Its low-Mach lift measurements support a candidate near 103 mph EAS at 9,240 lb. This panel tests that candidate through the shared solver; it does not change the general flight-model assumptions above.

Source conditions and remaining limits

The P-51B source had carefully filled, waxed and polished surfaces. Transfer to 43-24777 and its propeller state remains conditional. The source uses 233.2 sqft; the solver's 233 sqft convention is reconciled by preserving lift coefficient times area. Mach and altitude restrictions prevent using this low-speed coefficient for unrestricted turn predictions.

Spitfire RAE measurements of 1.36 glide / 1.89 full throttle concern an earlier mark. Its 91.9 mph EAS transfer at 7,110 lb is a sensitivity, not a verified IX stall. P-47B's original calibration starts at 170 IAS. Bf109 F1/F2 landing-speed distinctions do not establish a clean F4 stall.
