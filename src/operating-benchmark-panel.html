Aircraft settings, load assumptions and sources

Normal and Combat use the same service-load reconstruction. No bombs, rockets, external tanks or optional gun pods; removable racks excluded. A charged injection reservoir is carried in dry and injected cases. Full-ammo weights and some lower-rating/altitude power conversions remain qualified engineering reconstructions.
